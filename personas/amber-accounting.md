# Persona: Amber (Accounting / Purchasing)

**Who:** Amber reviews what the crews submit. Detail-oriented, cares about whether
numbers are trustworthy and whether the approval trail is clear. Desktop user.

**Login:** test account (logs in herself when prompted).

**Primary goal:** Review counts/tasks the crews submitted and approve (or reject)
them through the task/approval workflow.

**Believable path:**
1. Go to the review/approval queue (counts or tasks awaiting approval).
2. Open a submitted item and inspect the detail — does she have enough info to
   decide without chasing someone down?
3. Approve at least one item.
4. Reject or send back at least one item (if the workflow supports it) and see what
   happens to it.
5. Confirm the state actually changed and is reflected where the submitter would
   see it (data-integrity / workflow-consistency check — record it).

**Features this persona must cover:** task/approval workflow, review detail view,
approve action, reject/send-back action, status reflection after action.

**What Amber cares about (capture in `felt`):** is the queue clear, does she have
enough context to approve confidently, does the status update reliably, is there an
audit trail.
